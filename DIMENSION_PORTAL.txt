✨ NEW MECHANIC: DIMENSION PORTAL & BOSS ENCOUNTER

🎮 HOW IT WORKS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. PORTAL SPAWNS (at 20 seconds into game)
   • Purple neon glowing circle appears
   • Pulses with energy
   • Slowly drifts downward
   • Has 5 HP (requires 5 clicks to destroy)

2. ANOMALY PHASE
   • Click the portal to deal damage
   • Each hit triggers a glitch effect
   • Sparks and particles fly
   • Portal becomes more unstable as HP decreases
   • Game continues normally with enemies spawning

3. PORTAL DESTRUCTION → DIMENSION SHIFT
   • Screen flashes white (transition effect)
   • Background changes to darker purple tone (#1a0033)
   • All enemies disappear
   • Boss emerges in the alternate dimension

4. BOSS ENCOUNTER
   • Magenta star-shaped boss appears
   • Has 20 HP (requires 20 clicks to defeat)
   • Performs attacks and moves around
   • Rewards 100 points per hit + combo multiplier
   • Defeating boss grants +500 bonus points

5. RETURN TO NORMAL
   • After boss defeated
   • Screen flashes white again
   • Returns to normal dimension
   • Regular enemies resume spawning
   • Portal system resets for next cycle
