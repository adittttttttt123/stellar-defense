⚡ QUICK START GUIDE ⚡

1️⃣  OPEN THE GAME
   ➜ Simply open index.html in your web browser
   ➜ Or open in VS Code and use Live Server extension

2️⃣  PLAY THE GAME
   ➜ Click "PLAY GAME" button on menu
   ➜ Move mouse to aim the red crosshair
   ➜ Click enemies to destroy them
   ➜ Don't let enemies reach the bottom!

3️⃣  SCORING
   ➜ UFO: 10 points
   ➜ Meteor: 15 points
   ➜ Alien Tentacle: 25 points
   ➜ Multiply by combo (1x-5x) for bigger scores!

4️⃣  COMBO SYSTEM
   ➜ Kill enemies quickly to build combo multiplier
   ➜ Combo increases for each kill (max 5x)
   ➜ Resets after 2 seconds of no kills
   ➜ Higher combo = Much higher scores!

5️⃣  CONTROLS
   🖱️  Mouse: Move to aim, click to shoot
   📱 Touch: Works on mobile devices
   ⌨️  SPACE: Pause/Resume
   ⌨️  ESC: Quit to menu

6️⃣  GAMEPLAY TIPS
   • Focus on Alien Tentacles (most points)
   • Keep your combo multiplier active
   • Watch for enemies about to reach bottom
   • Difficulty increases over time
   • Good combos are the key to high scores!

7️⃣  GAME OVER
   ➜ Happens when you lose all 3 hearts
   ➜ See your final score, time, and max combo
   ➜ Click "PLAY AGAIN" to retry
   ➜ Try to beat your previous high score!

❓ TROUBLESHOOTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Problem: Game doesn't load
Solution: Make sure all files are in the same folder

Problem: Black screen
Solution: Check browser console (F12) for errors

Problem: Enemies invisible
Solution: Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)

Problem: Controls unresponsive
Solution: Click on the canvas first, then use controls

Problem: Performance laggy
Solution: Close other tabs, try different browser
