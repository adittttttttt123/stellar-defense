📊 SCORING SYSTEM
──────────────────
UFO Kill:           10 × combo = points
Meteor Kill:        15 × combo = points
Alien Tentacle:     25 × combo = points

Combo Multiplier:
  Kill 1: ×1
  Kill 2: ×2 (within 2 seconds)
  Kill 3: ×3 (within 2 seconds)
  Kill 4: ×4 (within 2 seconds)
  Kill 5: ×5 (capped at 5x)
  
Reset:              2 seconds of no kills

Example: Kill sequence in 2 seconds
  10 (UFO) + 15×2 (Meteor) + 25×3 (Alien) = 10 + 30 + 75 = 115 points!

⚙️ DIFFICULTY SCALING
──────────────────────
Time  │ Multiplier │ Spawn Behavior
──────┼────────────┼──────────────────
0s    │ 1.0x       │ Normal spawn rate
30s   │ 1.5x       │ Enemies appear 50% faster

Spawn Rates (base, modified by difficulty):

🚀 HOW TO PLAY
────────────────

3. Move mouse to aim crosshair
