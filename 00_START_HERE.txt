🚀 HOW TO PLAY

1. OPEN THE GAME
   → Open index.html in your web browser
   → Should load instantly

2. START PLAYING
   → Click "PLAY GAME" button
   → Game begins immediately

3. AIM & SHOOT
   → Move mouse to position crosshair
   → Click enemies to destroy them

4. BUILD COMBOS
   → Kill enemies quickly to build combo
   → Combo up to 5x = massive points!

5. SURVIVE
   → Don't let enemies reach bottom
   → Lose hearts when they escape
   → Game ends at 0 hearts

6. SCORE POINTS
   → UFO: 10 × combo
   → Meteor: 15 × combo
   → Alien: 25 × combo

7. GAME OVER
   → View final statistics
   → Click "PLAY AGAIN" to retry
